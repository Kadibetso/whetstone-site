Last updated 25 September 2026.

Bookings

Appointments can be requested through this website, by phone or by email. A booking is only confirmed once you receive confirmation from CROWN & CUT BARBERS by phone, SMS or email. We recommend arriving five minutes before your appointment time.

Cancellations & rescheduling

We ask for at least four hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Cancellations made with less notice, or repeated no-shows, may be asked to pay a deposit for future bookings.

Late arrivals

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten your service or move your appointment to the next available slot to avoid delaying other customers.

Pricing

Prices listed on this website are current at time of publishing and may change without notice. The price confirmed at the time of booking or on arrival is the price charged.

Under-18 customers

Customers under 18 must be accompanied by, or have the consent of, a parent or guardian.

Liability

CROWN & CUT BARBERS takes reasonable care with every service. Please tell your barber about any skin conditions, allergies or sensitivities before your service begins. We are not liable for reactions arising from undisclosed conditions.

Privacy

Information you provide when booking (name, phone, email) is used only to manage your appointment and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to book@whetstonebarber.co.za.
