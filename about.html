Our story

CROWN & CUT BARBERS opened in 2016 in a converted hardware store on Foundry Lane, and we kept more of the building than we meant to — the timber counter, the shelving, and a habit that became our name. A CROWN & CUT BARBERS sits by the basin at every chair, and every straight razor gets a few passes on it before it touches a face. It's slower than buying pre-sharpened blades. We think you can tell the difference.

A decade on, the shop is still built around that same idea: do the unglamorous part properly, and the haircut takes care of itself. We keep three chairs, no more, so the people who work here know their regulars by name and their fades by memory.
